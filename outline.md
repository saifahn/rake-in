# Outline of app

## Goal

- Comparing potential rental properties in Japan
- There are all kinds of different costs for each property - the monthly rent is not the only thing
  - There's various insurances we need to pay, sometimes companies have their own support insurance thing
  - Properties have non-refundable "key money"
  - Some properties have renewal fees every two years
- I want to be able to compare properties and get a quick overview of the cost difference between each one at certain points - 1, 2, 3 years because:
  - non-refundable key money counts less towards a monthly rent the longer you live there
  - I'm not sure how long we will be living at a certain place
  - I want to spot any potential big differences
